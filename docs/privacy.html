Privacy & Data Collection

What the app collects, in plain terms.

Easy Line Up can optionally send anonymous, aggregate usage statistics. This isn't hidden in fine print — here's exactly what that means.

How you decide: if you installed via the Windows installer, you saw this notice during Setup and your choice there is final — no separate in-app prompt appears. If you got the app another way, a short one-time screen appears on first launch instead, with Agree / No Thanks buttons. Either way, nothing changes after that first decision — there's no ongoing tracking toggle to manage.

What is NOT collected

  • Your name, email, Steam ID, or CS2 in-game identity
  • Your IP address (discarded after deriving country)
  • Anything about your gameplay, aim, position, or in-match behavior
  • Any of your lineup content, notes, or images
  • Anything typed into the app

Where it goes

Events are sent to PostHog, a third-party analytics service, over HTTPS. Their own privacy policy governs how they handle data in transit and at rest: posthog.com/privacy.
